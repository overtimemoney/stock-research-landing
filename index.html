The idea

20% every year is Buffett territory. 15% starts with knowing what to buy—and what not to buy.

Stock Research will not promise impossible returns. It gives you a repeatable way to inspect value, growth, risk, and earnings durability before excitement takes over.

The goal is not more noise. It is a clearer shortlist, a visible reason for every conclusion, and a record of what you believed when you made the decision.

Inside the app

Research tools that show their work.

Start with a stock, a daily shortlist, or your own portfolio. Every route leads back to the same question: is the possible reward worth the risk?

Scorecard

A transparent verdict, factor by factor

See valuation, growth, profitability, financial strength, and risk inputs laid out clearly—not hidden inside a single unexplained rating.

Value screen

The 4-criteria value test

Run every candidate through the same four-part screen, so “cheap” has a definition and exciting stories still have to qualify.

Earnings risk

A 3-year EPS cliff check

Look beyond the next earnings jump. The cliff check highlights when forecast growth may fade sharply after a temporary peak.

Daily shortlist

Top 10 value + low risk, daily

Start the day with a focused list that balances valuation and safety instead of asking you to scan thousands of tickers.

Projection Builder

Future earnings, mapped to a price

Lay out a stock’s earnings year by year and see what price those earnings could support, with Bear and Bull cases next to your base case.

Cloud sync

Pick up where you left off

Create an optional account to sync your watchlist, portfolio, journal, saved projections, and research across devices.
